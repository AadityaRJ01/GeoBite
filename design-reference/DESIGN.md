## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an editorial, print-inspired digital travel companion designed for discerning travelers, culinary explorers, and cultural flâneurs. It bridges the tangible warmth of independent travel journals with the quiet precision of modern geospatial technology.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Editorial Serenity:** Evoking publications like *Monocle* and *Kinfolk*, interfaces favor intentional negative space, measured proportions, and tactile paper-like backdrops over dense UI dashboards.
- **Organic Precision:** Curated itinerary planning is framed through natural, grounded tonality—avoiding synthetic tech-neon gradients or clinical utilitarian SaaS aesthetics.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Layouts balance asymmetrical editorial framing with rhythmic geospatial data blocks, treating destination discovery as an intimate cultural immersion.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in an earthy, high-contrast spectrum derived from coastal botanicals, ancient stone pathways, and unbleached stationery.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0E2A24`):** Deep primeval forest green. Used for authoritative typography, primary navigation structures, dominant interactive states, and key wayfinding anchors.
- **Secondary (`#2A7E72`):** Coastal eucalyptus. Bridges the dark pine base with interactive highlights, map pins, active itinerary milestones, and verified badge treatments.
- **Tertiary (`#48A999`):** Soft tidal teal. Reserved for secondary interactive cues, hover state blooms, category filters, and itinerary stop sequence accents.
- **Neutral Core (`#19211E`):** Charcoal slate. Ensures maximum typographic contrast without the harsh, sterile edge of absolute black (`#000000`).
- **Canvas & Surface Tones:**
  - Base Ground: Warm ivory eggshell (`#FBFBFA`)
  - Elevated Container Ground: Linen chalk (`#F5F5F0`)
  - Muted Borders & Dividers: Warm stone sand (`#E6E4DF`)
  - Subdued Body Copy: Slate timber (`#4A5550`)

## Typography

The type scale juxtaposes literary European serifs with crisp, geometry-driven modern grotesques.

### Typographic Roles
- **Playfair Display:** Establishes editorial authority across hero destinations, narrative intros, and culinary story titles. Its delicate horizontal hairlines and steep contrast convey hospitality and heritage.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Governs body narratives, operational mechanics, routing details, timestamps, and interactive controls. Its open apertures preserve legibility across responsive map layers and multi-tiered itinerary columns.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a fluid 12-column grid that mirrors curated magazine spreads rather than standard app dashboards.

### Breakpoints & Geometry
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Margin collapses to `1.25rem` (`margin-mobile`), gutter shrinks to `1rem` (`gutter-mobile`). Bottom sheets and swipeable horizontal cards replace split-screen panes.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Margin expands to `2rem`, gutter to `1.25rem`. Map views pair alongside stacked itinerary lists.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout. Margin expands to `3rem`, gutter rests at `1.5rem`. Split-screen architecture dominates: sticky interactive cartography on one side (5 columns), narrative itinerary cards and discovery collections on the other (7 columns).
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8px architectural grid. Component layouts prioritize generous internal breathability (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) around editorial photography to avoid visual crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering relies on atmospheric, multi-layered ambient diffusion and surface-tier stacking, rejecting blunt drop shadows and flat material borders.

### Depth System
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Unbleached eggshell (`#FBFBFA`) flat baseline canvas.
- **Level 1 (Curated Cards & Waypoints):** Linen chalk surface (`#F5F5F0`) paired with a razor-thin structural border (`1px solid #E6E4DF`) and a diffused botanical shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(14, 42, 36, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Map Markers & Hovered Stops):** Raised card floating over interactive maps. Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(14, 42, 36, 0.08)`, accented by a delicate upper highlight border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`).
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Route Curators):** Floating navigation pills, sticky booking bars, and filter sheets. Elevated with `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(14, 42, 36, 0.12)` alongside a subtle backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with 85% opacity `#FBFBFA` surface).

## Shapes

The design system embraces an organic, generous contour language that softens technical map telemetry and aligns with bespoke publication layouts.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full`):** Reserved for category filters, status chips, search floating pills, step-by-step route sequences, and primary call-to-action buttons.
- **Generous Containers (`rounded-3xl` / 2rem - 3rem):** Used for venue showcase cards, hero photography frames, modal bottom sheets, and route summary modules.
- **Micro Surfaces (`rounded-xl` / 0.75rem - 1rem):** Input text fields, mini route cards, time badges, and transit icon badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#0E2A24` background, `#FBFBFA` text. Generous internal padding (`0.875rem 2rem`). Subtle scale depression (`0.98`) on active tap.
- **Secondary Outline:** Pill-shaped, transparent background, `1.5px solid #2A7E72`, `#0E2A24` text. On hover, background washes with `rgba(42, 126, 114, 0.08)`.
- **Tertiary Minimal:** Text-only in `#2A7E72` with an understated underlined border that expands outward on hover.

### Chips & Category Filters
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), compact height (`36px`), styled in `#F5F5F0` with a `1px solid #E6E4DF` boundary.
- Active state transitions background to `#0E2A24`, text to `#FBFBFA`, eliminating border tension.

### Itinerary Stop & Venue Cards
- Dual-zone cards wrapped in `#F5F5F0` with `rounded-3xl` corners and `1px solid #E6E4DF` framing.
- Imagery occupies 55% of visual weight, styled with edge-to-edge bleed or inset margins framed like fine-art prints.
- Typography stacks: Stop order indicator (`label-sm` in `#48A999`), Venue title (`headline-sm` in `#0E2A24`), and concise experiential review (`body-sm` in `#4A5550`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Custom rounded circles for radios and `rounded-md` for checks.
- Unselected: Crisp `1.5px solid #E6E4DF` border on canvas ivory.
- Selected: Solid `#0E2A24` fill with an off-white `#FBFBFA` checkmark or center pip.

### Input Fields
- Floating, pill-inspired or soft-cornered (`rounded-2xl`) containers with background `#F5F5F0` and `1px solid #E6E4DF`.
- Inset label pattern transitioning to `#4A5550` on focus; border smooth-shifts to `#2A7E72` without harsh focus rings.

### Specialized Travel-Tech Components
- **Multi-Stop Route Connectors:** Vertical or horizontal dotted stone guidelines (`#E6E4DF`) linking sequentially numbered circular pills (`#0E2A24` with teal index indicators), conveying walking or driving transit intervals between hidden gems.
- **Atmospheric Map Clusters:** Circular ivory markers encased in low-diffusion forest shadows, pulsing softly in `#48A999` to highlight proximity to current user waypoints.

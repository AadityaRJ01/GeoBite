Cartographic Philosophy

Why Planar Untangling Matters for Gastronomic Travelers

Standard traveling salesperson heuristics often treat terrain as a Euclidean plane. In places like San Sebastián and the Basque corniche, pedestrian routing contends with the Urumea river estuary, Mount Igueldo funicular barriers, and the narrow historic grid of Parte Vieja. 2-Opt systematically prevents travelers from crossing the same bridge multiple times or retracing steep steps between pincho bars.
